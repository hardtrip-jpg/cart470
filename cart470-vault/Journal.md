# Sept 22, 2026

I'm writing this quite later then I should be. It's currently 11pm, and I have to wake up in less then 8 hours. This is due to the fact I've spent the last 3 hours researching how to make a multiband stereo widener, and more specifically what Ozone's widening algorithm is. It's been quite fun, but useless to this specific project. The reason I bring it up is due to the fact that I've been the most interested in music I've been in years. During the first team discussions, I immediately brought up my intentions to learn FMOD through whatever project we received. Not only is it where I'd be most proficient, but it's also where my interests most lie. 

Since receiving the AbTec project, I've been uncertain in my potential usefulness. First off, I'm a cis white quebecois. I am culturally very detached from this project, in a way that my involvement might uninvertadly be offensive. Though I have lot of experience in project management, especially when it comes to software development, I can't see myself contributing meaningfully to this projects design. Secondly, there is so much uncertainty in what we actually have to design. During the team discussion, we hunkered down and jotted down all the potential avenues we could potential fall in. Maybe some platform the gallery could use that would allow them to step away from Second Life? Maybe it's just an unreal version of the gallery and we're thinking too hard? Or do they want to target web? Or increase player interactivity? What do they want, we don't know. My personal hope is platform, and forcing my team to use the Godot game engine. Alex seems to be the most hesitant, which is completely fair considering his experience is primarily with Unity. Nadia would like to use Unreal, but Unreal brings a lot of heavyness that I'd like to avoid. I'd also like to avoid Unity not only due to my personal disdain with the engine, but the engines immorality these past few years. For this type of project, I'd like to be a lot more conscious about the tech we choose. This was also shared by Bea, in fact it was initially framed by them this way and I'm just stealing their perspective. 

The third, and final big part of this project is, "How important is Second Life to this experience?". AbTec gallery has always existed in Second Life, and it has existed for a long time. When Second Life initially came out, it offered a novelty that was unmatched. A fully customizable digital space that you could share with others. Not only could you customize the space, but you could customize yourself and have other interpret you however you wanted them too. Second Life provided a genuine escapist experience, so having a gallery that ties itself back to cultural reality was very striking. Nowadays, Second Life's novelty exists in a plethera of other offerings, most famously VRChat. I'm wondering if the gallery wants to move to VRChat. It would make this project very belittling (in the sense that it would be incredibly easy and half of us would be out of work), however it would make the most sense. Occams Razor and all.

Personally, I think the best option for this project is some custom Blender scripts, and a repackaged Godot project that exports and updated gallery project to web. Something simple and tweakable that the gallery can use to generate itself. This would leave us out of the equation as much as possible, and leave the gallery to .... i just tried typing in 4 different words and I don't know which to use ... none are good. I hope the idea is passed through anyways.

# Sept 29, 2026

Again, as last week, I am writing this much too late. Worse even, I am sick and its been a generally rough week for me. Reflecting on my journal last week, I genuinely don't have much to add for this week. Our meeting with ABTeC went more or less as expected, considering the main person in charge was not there. There is still much uncertainty in what ABTeC wants, but at least the uncertainty is codeified. I'm excited to get the chance to use Godot again, even though its unlikely we will stick with it. The meeting did bring up certain pain points I'm not quite excited for. 

For one, GenAI was mentioned. I understand the technology is new and inviting, but as a young person with a staunch anti-capitalist position, I hate having to acknowledge it. Mainly due to the fact that the people who push it have very vapid and isolating views of the world. Either way I understand new technologies are always interesting and should be researched, but with the current state of things I just can't see myself to even working around it. Keep in mind, I don't use google services like docs, I use Cryptpad. I haven't used windows in 3 years (or apple in 2), I don't use social media (other then bluesky since thats where the Godot community is most active), and I even switched out of Proton due the CEOs pro-trump takes back during the last voting season. I've felt its been too easy for many to abandon their morals for technology, or at least ignore them. It's always the excuse of "its the future", as some detachment of their own individual contribution in establishing what is a very dystopic future. So when AI is mentioned, it triggers my hyper-awareness of the tech space. Same with unreal, same with unity. The world is so unfair, and there are so many better alternatives. Especially considering the 'tool' in question will likely lead to atrophy. https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08872 

Secondly, as previously mentioned, Skawennati was not at the meeting. However, one of the major points brought up was the 'look' of the gallery. The word pretty was thrown around. I understand what the word pretty means, I get the context. I understand and agree that Unreal makes thing look pretty easily. However, what even is the point of pretty. One of the fascinating things about ABTeC is the constraints it lives in. Over the years, the team at ABTeC have had to problem solve and find so many unique workarounds to problems that don't exist in the real world. Digital spaces offer limitless possibilities, and yet here, we exist in an incredibly rule heavy world where things can only work a certain way. To me personally, that's a major factor to this gallery. The reason I believe this was achieved was due to the concrete goal Skawennati has of bringer her friends art into a digital space. Nothing more, nothing less. 'Pretty' is abstract. It's not a goal, it's a feeling. It doesn't communicate anything, it's just sort of their. 

There is beauty, and there is pretty.

I am worried that this project will fail because we left the beauty of the gallery behind for pretty.

Anyways, those are my thoughts. Just kind of been in a sick angry mood listening to They Might Be Giants and Aquabats in an attempt to cheer me up.
