Or do they want to target web? Or increase player interactivity? What do they want, we don't know. My personal hope is platform, and forcing my team to use the Godot game engine. For this type of project, I'd like to be a lot more conscious about the tech we choose. This was also shared by Bea, in fact it was initially framed by them this way and I'm just stealing their perspective. 

The third, and final big part of this project is, "How important is Second Life to this experience?". AbTec gallery has always existed in Second Life, and it has existed for a long time. When Second Life initially came out, it offered a novelty that was unmatched. A fully customizable digital space that you could share with others. Not only could you customize the space, but you could customize yourself and have other interpret you however you wanted them too. Second Life provided a genuine escapist experience, so having a gallery that ties itself back to cultural reality was very striking. Nowadays, Second Life's novelty exists in a plethera of other offerings, most famously VRChat. I'm wondering if the gallery wants to move to VRChat. It would make this project very belittling (in the sense that it would be incredibly easy and half of us would be out of work), however it would make the most sense. Occams Razor and all.

Personally, I think the best option for this project is some custom Blender scripts, and a repackaged Godot project that exports and updated gallery project to web. Something simple and tweakable that the gallery can use to generate itself.
